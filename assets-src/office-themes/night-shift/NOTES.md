# night-shift — art direction

Name: en "Night shift" / vi "Ca đêm".

**Mood:** a calm, late, neon-lit studio — the office after hours, lit by monitors and desk lamps.

**Materials:** pale lavender-grey floor tiles, dark walnut wood, bluish glass, teal neon strips, white desks,
amber lamp shades, deep teal sofa with rose cushions.

**Key colours:**

| role | hex |
|---|---|
| deep navy (sky, shadows, outline) | `#1d2240` |
| indigo | `#3a3f7a` |
| teal glow | `#35d0c8` |
| rose accent (full-bleed art only, never in chroma assets) | `#e0559a` |
| warm lamp | `#f2b552` |
| pale lavender-grey wall | `#e6e8f4` |
| walnut | `#462d2f` |

**Light:** top-left; the night feel comes from colour only (navy grounds, glowing windows, lamp pools).

Room walls stay light (L ≥ 93 %); the night look in rooms comes from darker trims (navy, walnut, indigo),
mid-tone carpets (dusk lavender, slate, teal, walnut, amber) and saturated accents darkened to ≥ 3.9:1.
